Changed the Backstage template? Reload it

      
After editing service-template/template.yaml and re-running ./setup_gitops.sh, the portal keeps serving the old cached template. A pod restart is not enough — you must force a catalog refresh of the template's location, otherwise onboarding fails with a stale catalog-info.yaml register URL.

      
./reload-template.shclick to copy

      
Manual equivalent (needs a Backstage port-forward + guest token):

      
POST /api/catalog/refresh (location:…/platform-golden-path-templates)click to copy
